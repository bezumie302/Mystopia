## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Mystopia** is a delightful sandbox building game for Windows that invites players to craft serene and whimsical worlds at their own pace. With its enchanting isometric visuals and focus on creativity, **Mystopia** provides a stress-free gaming experience, allowing you to explore and engage in peaceful gameplay. The **latest version** is designed to foster creativity with a **complete functionality** that encourages endless exploration through various activities like potion mixing and unique environment construction.

In **Mystopia**, cheerful creatures known as Monis guide players with specific requests, adding purpose to the creative process. With three distinct play modes—Normal Mode, Saga Mode, and Creative Mode—players can tailor their experience to their preferences. The game features an extensive variety of blocks and potion effects, ensuring each building session feels fresh and engaging.
